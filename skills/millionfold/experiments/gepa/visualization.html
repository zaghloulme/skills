Evolutionary optimization of the Millionfold Protocol’s 14 tunable parameters. Best fitness climbs from 0.518 to 0.781 (+50.9%) over 30 generations, validated across 5 independent random seeds.

Cross-Seed Validation

Five independent runs (seeds 7, 13, 21, 99, 123) with 20 generations each confirm reproducibility. All seeds converge well above the v2 generation-1 baseline (0.518).
